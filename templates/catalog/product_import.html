How it works
Download the Excel template, add one item per row, and upload it. If a category does not exist, Gromaster creates it automatically. Existing categories are matched without case, spacing or punctuation differences. Gromaster generates each SKU from the category prefix. For example, Grains with prefix GR generates GR-003 after GR-001 and GR-002.

Required columns: Category, Item Name, Unit, Opening Quantity, Minimum Quantity, Description.
